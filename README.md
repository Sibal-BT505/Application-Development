# *Suits* (USA Network)

**Suits** is a sharp, fast-paced legal drama created by Aaron Korsh. Set in a high-powered New York City law firm, it blends high-stakes corporate litigation with witty banter and loyalty-driven drama.

---

## Core Premise

**Harvey Specter**, one of Manhattan's top corporate closers, takes a massive gamble by hiring **Mike Ross**—a brilliant college dropout with a photographic memory—as his associate, despite Mike never having attended law school.

* **The Secret**: Only a select few know Mike lacks a law degree, creating constant tension as they win cases while keeping their fraud hidden.
* **The Dynamic**: Harvey provides the ruthless confidence and street smarts, while Mike brings eidetic recall and genuine empathy for clients.

> "I don't play the odds, I play the man."  
> — *Harvey Specter*

## Why It Stands Out

1. **Rapid-Fire Dialogue**: Packed with pop-culture references, movie quotes, and razor-sharp legal comebacks (`Pearson Hardman`).
2. **Iconic Style**: Known for bespoke Tom Ford suits, sleek Manhattan offices, and a killer indie-rock soundtrack.
3. **Complex Rivalries**: **Louis Litt** evolves from an insecure antagonist into one of the most layered and lovable characters on television.
